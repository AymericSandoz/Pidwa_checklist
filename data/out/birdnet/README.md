# BirdNET V2.4 model files

These files are the BirdNET V2.4 acoustic classifier and its species range model, in TensorFlow.js format.
They are redistributed here unmodified, for a personal, non-commercial field checklist app.

- **Authors**: the BirdNET team, K. Lisa Yang Center for Conservation Bioacoustics at the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, and Chemnitz University of Technology.
- **Source**: https://github.com/birdnet-team/real-time-pwa (folder `public/models/birdnet`), itself derived from https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Analyzer
- **Licence**: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
  The BirdNET Live repository states CC BY-SA 4.0 for the same files; the stricter of the two statements is applied here.
- **Reference**: Kahl, S., Wood, C. M., Eibl, M., & Klinck, H. (2021). BirdNET: A deep learning solution for avian diversity monitoring. Ecological Informatics, 61, 101236.

`classes.json` is generated by `data/scripts/11_birdnet.mjs` from the BirdNET label files (English and French) and adds, for each class, the matching species of the Pidwa checklist when there is one.
